# anaBokra School Pilot — One-Pager

**A free closed beta for private secondary schools in Cairo and Alexandria · grades 10–11 · November to mid-December 2026**

## What anaBokra is

anaBokra (أنا بكرة, "I am tomorrow") is a bilingual Arabic/English career guidance app. Students complete a science-based assessment of their **interests** (based on O\*NET), **personality** (IPIP Big Five), **work values**, and **abilities**. It takes about 40 minutes, in **two short sittings** inside an island world that feels like a game, not an exam.

They then get **explained career matches** from an Egypt-first library of **82 careers** in 12 career groups, and can explore Egyptian study routes for each. Each student takes home a **printed results card** to discuss with their family.

anaBokra offers **guidance, not diagnosis**. It does not label students or decide anything for them. It gives them a structured, evidence-based starting point for a conversation with their family and counselor.

## Why it matters

- Around **920,000** students sit Thanaweya Amma in 2026. Most make decisions about faculty and career with little structured support.
- Guidance today is scattered: family opinion, tutoring-center advice, social media, and the final-year score. Counselors in most schools cannot give each student individual time.
- Students who understand their own interests and strengths in grades 10–11 can make choices earlier and with more confidence, before the final-year pressure starts.

## What the school gets

1. **Free participation** for all consenting students.
2. **Cohort insight report**: an anonymized view of your students' interest distribution, popular career and study directions, participation, and the change in career-decision readiness before and after.
3. **Counselor walkthrough**: a live session and a short guide on reading the results cards and running a follow-up conversation with students.
4. **Early-partner pricing** for the following school year, offered first to pilot schools.
5. A say in the product: your counselors' feedback shapes what we build next.

## What anaBokra gets (full transparency)

Feedback on the product, permission to quote school feedback (only with your approval), and anonymous pilot data to **validate the Arabic assessment** (reliability and norms), where parents agree. The Arabic assessment is **being validated with Egyptian students in this pilot**. We do not claim it is already validated.

## Data privacy promises

- **Parental consent first.** The school distributes our consent form. Students take part only with a signed parent/guardian consent, and students can always decline or skip questions.
- **No names with anaBokra.** Each student uses an ID and PIN. The list matching IDs to names stays at the school.
- **Minimal data.** We do not collect names, national ID numbers, dates of birth, phone numbers, addresses, or school grades.
- **Data stays on our tablets, in Egypt.** Nothing is sent to AI services or abroad. The tablets are encrypted and wiped after the pilot.
- **Individual results belong to the student and family.** The school receives only the **anonymized cohort report**. Small groups are never shown. The counselor gets a copy of a student's results card only if the parent opts in.
- **No selling, no ads, no sharing with third parties** for marketing. Research uses only anonymous data, reported in aggregate.
- **Withdrawal at any time** through the school, with deletion of the student's data from the tablet.
- Results are never used for grading, streaming, admission, or discipline.

*Tablets are wiped by 31 December 2026. Anonymous research data is deleted once the analysis is done, no later than 17 June 2027. Subject to lawyer review.*
