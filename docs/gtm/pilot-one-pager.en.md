# anaBokra University Pilot — One-Pager

**A free closed beta for universities and institutes in Cairo and Alexandria · students and recent graduates aged 18–25 · November to mid-December 2026**

## What anaBokra is

anaBokra (أنا بكرة, "I am tomorrow") is a bilingual Arabic/English career guidance app for young adults. Participants complete a science-based assessment of their **interests** (based on O\*NET), **personality** (IPIP Big Five), **work values** and **abilities**. It takes about 40 minutes in four short parts, inside an island world that feels like a game, not a test.

They then get **explained career matches** from an Egypt-first library of **82 careers** in 12 career groups, and explore the routes into each one in Egypt.

anaBokra offers **guidance, not diagnosis**. It doesn't label anyone or decide for them. It gives a structured, evidence-based starting point for decisions about a specialisation, a first job or a change of direction.

## Why it matters

- Many students choose a faculty by their score rather than by fit, and reach their final years unsure which careers suit them.
- Career centres can't give every student individual time. A structured self-assessment makes each conversation with a counsellor more useful.
- Graduates looking for a first job or a switch benefit from a clear picture of their interests, strengths and what they value at work.

## What the pilot involves

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | 3–5 universities or institutes in Cairo/Alexandria, about 300 participants in total, aged 18–25 |
| **When** | Kickoff in early November 2026; sessions late November to early December; finished by mid-December; report by 31 December |

| **Devices** | Participants' own phones. No installation or account needed; it runs in the browser |
| **Partner effort** | A named lead, a room with Wi-Fi, promoting the sessions through your channels, and running one results workshop |
| **anaBokra effort** | Materials, running the first session, support, staff walkthrough, and the report |

## What the partner gets

1. **Free participation** for your students and graduates.
2. **Partner insight report**: an anonymized view of participants' interest distribution, popular career directions, participation, and the change in career-decision readiness. Small groups are never shown.
3. **Staff walkthrough**: a live session and a short guide on reading results and running a follow-up conversation.
4. **Early-partner pricing** for the following academic year.
5. A say in the product: your team's feedback shapes what we build next.

## What anaBokra gets (full transparency)

Feedback on the product, permission to quote you (only with your approval), and anonymous data to **validate the Arabic assessment** (reliability and norms) from participants who choose to share it. The Arabic assessment is **being validated in this pilot**. We do not claim it is already validated.

## Data privacy promises

- **No accounts and no names.** Participants don't sign up and we don't collect names, phone numbers, emails or national ID numbers.
- **Data stays on the participant's phone.** Answers and results are calculated on the phone and are not sent to us or anyone else. No AI.
- **Research data only by choice.** A participant can choose to send their answers anonymously, with no name or ID, to help validate the Arabic assessment.
- **Your report is anonymized.** You see group results only, never an individual's.
- **No selling, no ads, no marketing.**
- **Delete any time** from the app.
