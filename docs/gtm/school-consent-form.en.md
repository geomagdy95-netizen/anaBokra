# Parent / Guardian Consent Form

## anaBokra Career Guidance Pilot — [School name], [Academic year 2026/2027]

Dear parent or guardian,

Our school is taking part in a free pilot of **anaBokra**, a career guidance app, for students in grades 10 and 11. We would like your permission for your child to take part. Participation is **voluntary**. Please read this form and decide freely.

### 1. What is anaBokra?

anaBokra is an Arabic/English app where students answer questions about their interests, personality, work values, and abilities. It takes about 40 minutes in total, in two sittings of about 25 minutes at school between [start date] and [end date]. Students use **tablets that anaBokra brings to the school**, not their own phones. The student then sees suggested careers and can explore Egyptian universities and programs. They take home a **printed results card**.

anaBokra gives **guidance, not a diagnosis**. It is not an exam, and it does not decide your child's future. It is not used for grades, class placement, admission, or discipline.

### 2. Why is the school running a pilot?

anaBokra is a new product. During this pilot, anaBokra is also being validated with Egyptian students: we study whether the Arabic questions work well. The school receives a summary report about the group as a whole.

### 3. How your child's identity is protected

- The school gives your child a **student ID and PIN**. The list matching IDs to names **stays at the school**. anaBokra never receives your child's name.
- We **do not** collect your child's name, national ID number, date of birth, phone number, email, home address, or school grades.
- Nothing is sent to an AI service, and no personal data leaves Egypt.

### 4. What information is collected?

**Kept on the anaBokra tablet, under your child's ID only:**
- grade, secondary track and language; gender only if your child chooses to give it (used only for Arabic grammar);
- answers to the assessment questions, resulting scores and career matches, and choices made in the app (such as saved careers or universities);
- short surveys on how clear and useful the experience was and how ready your child feels to make career decisions, before and after.

**Sent from the tablet:**
- **Totals for the school** (for example, how many students finished): always. These contain no information about any one student.
- **Only if you tick the research box in section 7:**
  - your child's answers **with no ID**, tagged only with school, grade and language, for studying the Arabic questions;
  - the before-and-after readiness answers, linked to each other by a random code (not the ID) so the change can be measured.

### 5. Who can see what?

| Who | What they see |
|---|---|
| **Your child** | Their own results on the tablet, and the printed results card |
| **You (parent/guardian)** | The printed results card your child brings home |
| **The school** | Only an **anonymized group report** (for example, how many students prefer which interest areas). Groups too small to protect identity are never shown. The counselor gets a copy of your child's results card **only if you tick that box in section 7** |
| **anaBokra team** | Data under an ID, never a name, only to run the pilot and improve the app |
| **Researchers working with anaBokra** | Anonymous data only, if you tick the research box. Findings are published only as group statistics |

We do not sell personal data, show advertising to students, or share personal data with third parties for marketing.

### 6. Storage and deletion

The tablets are owned by anaBokra, encrypted, and locked to the app. Each student's data opens only with their ID and PIN. All data on the tablets is **deleted by 31 December 2026**, after the results cards are printed. Anonymous research data is kept only until the analysis is finished, and **no later than 17 June 2027**. Group statistics from that analysis (which describe no one) may be kept. ### 8. Your rights and withdrawal

- Participation is voluntary. Declining has **no effect** on your child at school.
- Your child may skip any question or stop at any time, and will be asked to confirm they are willing to take part before starting.
- You may **withdraw your consent at any time** by telling the school counselor or writing to [anaBokra contact email / phone]. The school passes your child's ID to anaBokra, and we delete your child's data from the tablet **within 31 days after the school visit at which we receive your request**, and in any case by 31 December 2026. Anonymous data that was already sent cannot be traced back to your child, so it cannot be removed.
- Until the tablets are wiped, you may ask through the school to see or correct your child's data.
