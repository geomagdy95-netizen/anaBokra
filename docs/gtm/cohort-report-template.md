## Report rules (apply to every section)

- **Anonymized and aggregated only.** No student names, codes, or individual profiles.
- **Minimum group size.** Any group or category with fewer than [10 — founder to confirm] students is merged or hidden to prevent identification.
- Report exact counts and percentages from the data. No rounding that overstates results. Where the sample is small, say so.
- The report is guidance for the school, not an evaluation of students or teachers.
