# anaBokra School Pilot Agreement

**Between:** [anaBokra legal entity name], [Commercial Register No.], [address] ("anaBokra")
**And:** [School name], [registration], [address], represented by [name, title] ("the School")
**Date:** [date]

## 1. Purpose
The School takes part in a free pilot of anaBokra, a career guidance app, for students in grades 10 and 11. The pilot helps anaBokra improve the app and study the Arabic version of its questions, and gives the School an anonymized report about its students as a group.

## 2. Dates
From [start date] to [end date], 2026. Student sittings take place on visit days agreed at the kickoff meeting, within the School's calendar.

## 3. What anaBokra does
1. Provides the parent consent form, parent message, one-pager and counselor guide, in Arabic and English.
2. Prepares random student IDs and PINs, registers students for visits, and sends the School a list of students per visit (IDs only).
3. Brings its own tablets to every visit, with a team member present, and runs the sittings.
4. Prints a results card for each student (ID only) to take home.
5. Trains the counselor (one session of about 60 minutes) and provides talking points for the results session.
6. Delivers an anonymized cohort report by [31 December 2026]. Groups with fewer than [10] students are merged or hidden.
7. Offers the School early-partner pricing for the following school year, with no obligation.
8. Bears all costs of the pilot.

## 4. What the School does
1. Names a counselor or coordinator as the contact for the pilot.
2. Sends the consent form to parents, collects the signed forms and **keeps them at the School**.
3. Assigns the IDs and PINs to consenting students, keeps the ID ↔ name list **at the School only**, and gives anaBokra only each student's ID, grade and the parent's optional choices.
4. Provides a quiet room and free class periods on visit days, and supervises the room.
5. Makes sure only students with a signed consent take part. Students who do not take part are not disadvantaged in any way.
6. Leads one results session with the printed cards.
7. Tells anaBokra, by student ID, when a parent withdraws consent or a student leaves.
8. Names a staff member as the contact for any student who is upset during or after the pilot: [name, role].

## 5. Data
1. anaBokra handles student data as described in the consent form and the anaBokra Privacy Policy (version [0.2]). In short: no names, national ID numbers, phone numbers or grades are collected; data stays on anaBokra's encrypted tablets and is deleted by 31 December 2026; only totals, and anonymous research data where the parent agreed, leave the tablets.
2. The School receives only the anonymized cohort report, and a copy of a student's results card only where the parent ticked that box. The School will not try to identify individual students from the report.
3. Neither party sells student data or uses it for advertising or marketing.
4. **Roles:** [LAWYER: separate controllers / joint controllers / School as controller and anaBokra as processor, and the clauses that follow from that choice.]
5. If a tablet is lost or data is exposed, anaBokra tells the School within [24 hours] and handles any notification required by law.

## 6. Results are guidance
Results are guidance for students, not a diagnosis or an evaluation of students or teachers. The School will not use them for grades, class placement, admission or discipline.

## 7. Quotes and testimonials
anaBokra may quote the School or name it as a pilot partner only with the School's written approval of the exact text. Quotes from students are used only anonymously and only with [parent consent — LAWYER].

## 8. Ending
Either party may end the pilot at any time with written notice. If it ends early, anaBokra deletes the School's data from the tablets within [7] days and still delivers a report if enough students took part.
