# anaBokra University Pilot — Partner Agreement

**Between:** [anaBokra legal entity name], [Commercial Register No.], [address] ("anaBokra")
**And:** [University/Institute], [unit: career centre / student activities office / club], [address], represented by [name, title] ("the Partner")
**Date:** [date]

## 1. Purpose
The Partner takes part in a free pilot of anaBokra, a career guidance app, for its students and recent graduates aged 18–25. The pilot helps anaBokra improve the app and study the Arabic version of its questions, and gives the Partner an anonymized report about participants as a group.

## 2. Dates
From [start date] to [end date], 2026. Sessions take place on dates agreed at the kickoff meeting.

## 3. What anaBokra does
1. Provides the session materials, promotion text, staff guide and results-workshop talking points, in Arabic and English.
2. Issues an event code for each Partner session.
3. Runs the first session (about 60 minutes) with a team member present, and supports participants with their phones.
4. Trains the Partner's staff (one session of about 60 minutes).
5. Delivers an anonymized partner report by [31 December 2026]. Groups with fewer than [10] participants are merged or hidden.
6. Offers early-partner pricing for the following academic year, with no obligation.
7. Bears all costs of the pilot.

## 4. What the Partner does
1. Names a lead for the pilot.
2. Promotes the sessions through its channels using anaBokra's text, and books a room with Wi-Fi.
3. Hosts the sessions and sends one reminder for the parts done at home.
4. Leads one results workshop.
5. Names a contact for any participant who is upset during or after the pilot: [name, role, e.g. student counselling].
6. Tells anaBokra in the first week whether a research or ethics approval is needed for the research option, and helps obtain it.

## 5. Participation and data
1. Participation is voluntary and open to people aged 18 or over. Each participant accepts anaBokra's Terms of Use and Privacy Policy (version [0.3]) in the app. **[LAWYER: ages 18–20.]**
2. Participants use their own phones. Their answers and results stay on their phones. anaBokra does not collect names, phone numbers, emails or ID numbers.
3. Participants may choose to share their answers **anonymously** for research. Only then are anonymous answers, and readiness answers linked by a random code, sent to anaBokra, tagged with the Partner's event code.
4. The Partner receives only the anonymized partner report and will not try to identify individual participants from it. The Partner receives no individual data.
5. Neither party sells participant data or uses it for advertising or marketing.
6. **Roles:** [LAWYER: anaBokra as controller of the anonymous research data; the Partner's role, if any.]

## 6. Results are guidance
Results are guidance for participants, not a diagnosis or an evaluation. The Partner will not use them for grades, admission, hiring or discipline.

## 7. Quotes and testimonials
anaBokra may quote the Partner or name it as a pilot partner only with the Partner's written approval of the exact text. Quotes from participants are used only anonymously and only with their consent.

## 8. Ending
Either party may end the pilot at any time with written notice. If it ends early, anaBokra still delivers a report if enough people took part.
