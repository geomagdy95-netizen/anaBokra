# Your Data on anaBokra — a short guide for teens

**Version 0.2-draft** · Read this in 2 minutes. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

## What is anaBokra?
anaBokra helps you find careers that fit you. You answer questions about what you like, how you think and what matters to you. The app turns that into career ideas and a map of islands you can explore.

## Where do your answers go?
**Nowhere.** On your phone, your answers, results and profile stay **on your phone**. The app works them out right there. We don't get them, so we can't see them.

- No account, no phone number, no email.
- No AI. The explanations were written by people in advance.
- No ads, and we don't follow you around the internet.

## Why do we need your parent or guardian?
If you are under 18, a parent or guardian has to say "yes" first. The app asks you to hand them the phone so they can read what anaBokra does and confirm. Nothing is saved until they do.

## Who can see your results?
| Who | What they see |
|---|---|
| **You** | Everything, in the app |
| **Anyone who uses your phone** | Your results too, so be careful who you lend it to |
| **anaBokra** | Nothing from your phone |

## Want to delete it?
Go to *Settings → Delete all my data*. It's gone straight away. If you delete the app or lose your phone, your results are gone too, and we can't bring them back.

## In the school pilot
If your school is in the anaBokra pilot, it works a bit differently:
- You use a **tablet we bring**, with a **student ID and PIN** instead of your name. Only your school knows which ID is yours.
- Your answers stay on that tablet. All tablets are wiped by **31 December 2026**.
- You get a **printed results card** to take home.
- Your school only sees a **group report** about everyone together, not your own results (unless your parent agreed that your counselor gets a copy of your card).
- If your parent agreed, your answers are also sent to us **without your ID**, so we can check that the Arabic questions work well.

## Remember
Your results are **ideas to explore, not a diagnosis and not a rule for your life.** You can skip any question and stop any time.

## If something feels wrong
Talk to a parent, teacher or another adult you trust. If you feel very down or unsafe, don't wait: call a trusted adult or [the child helpline — number to be verified].
