# Your data on anaBokra, in short

**Version 0.7-draft** · Read this in 2 minutes. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

## What is anaBokra?
anaBokra helps you find careers that fit you. You answer questions about what you like, how you think and what matters to you at work. The app turns that into career ideas and a map of islands you can explore.

## Where do your answers go?
**Nowhere.** Your answers, results and profile stay **on your phone, encrypted**. The app works them out right there. We don't get them, so we can't see them.

- No account, no email, no AI.
- No ads, and we don't follow you around the internet.

## If you're 15–17
You can use anaBokra on your own. We never ask for your name, phone number or ID, and nothing about you reaches us. It's a good idea to talk about your results with a parent or someone you trust.

## Who can see your results?
| Who | What they see |
|---|---|
| **You** | Everything, in the app |
| **Anyone who uses your phone** | Your results too, unless you lock the app with a password |
| **anaBokra** | Nothing from your phone, unless you agree to share anonymous statistics (below) |
| **Your university, at a pilot session** | Only a group report about everyone together, never your own results |

## Anonymous statistics, only if you say yes
If you agree, the app sends one short summary when you finish (your age group, stage, language, top interest areas and career groups, and score ranges), how each career path you check came out, your 1–5 rating of your results if you give one, and your first score in each game (to compare players). **No name, no phone number, no ID, nothing that points to you.** You can turn it off in Settings. At university pilot sessions, adults can also choose to share their answers anonymously for research.

## New phone?
Save a **backup file** locked with a passphrase and open it on the new phone. It never passes through us, and we can't open it. Keep your passphrase safe; we can't recover it.

## Want to delete it?
Use *Delete all my data*. It's gone straight away. If you delete the app or lose your phone without a backup, your results are gone too.

## Remember
Your results are **ideas to explore, not a diagnosis.** You can skip any question and stop any time.

## If something feels wrong
Talk to someone you trust. If you feel very down or unsafe, don't wait: call [a verified helpline — number to be confirmed].
