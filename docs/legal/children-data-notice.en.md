# Your data on anaBokra, in short

**Version 0.3-draft** · Read this in 2 minutes. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

## What is anaBokra?
anaBokra helps you find careers that fit you. You answer questions about what you like, how you think and what matters to you at work. The app turns that into career ideas and a map of islands you can explore.

## Where do your answers go?
**Nowhere.** Your answers, results and profile stay **on your phone**. The app works them out right there. We don't get them, so we can't see them.

- No account, no phone number, no email, no name.
- No AI. The explanations were written by people in advance.
- No ads, and we don't follow you around the internet.

## Who can see your results?
| Who | What they see |
|---|---|
| **You** | Everything, in the app |
| **Anyone who uses your phone** | Your results too, so be careful who you lend it to |
| **anaBokra** | Nothing from your phone, unless you choose to share anonymous research data (below) |
| **Your university, at a pilot session** | Only a group report about everyone together, never your own results |

## Research data, only if you say yes
At a pilot session, the app asks if you want to help us check that the Arabic questions work well. If you say yes, your answers are sent **without your name or any ID**. Your readiness answers before and after are linked by a random code made on your phone, so we can see how much they changed. We delete this data once the research is done, by 17 June 2027 at the latest. If you say no, nothing changes in the app.

## Want to delete it?
Use *Delete all my data*. It's gone straight away. If you delete the app or lose your phone, your results are gone too, and we can't bring them back.

## Remember
Your results are **ideas to explore, not a diagnosis.** You can skip any question and stop any time.

## If something feels wrong
Talk to someone you trust. If you feel very down or unsafe, don't wait: call [a verified helpline — number to be confirmed].
