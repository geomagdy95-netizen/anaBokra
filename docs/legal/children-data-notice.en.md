# Your Data on anaBokra — a short guide for teens

**Version 0.1-draft** · Read this in 2 minutes. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

## What is anaBokra?
anaBokra helps you find careers that fit you. You answer questions about what you like, how you think and what matters to you. We turn that into career ideas, opportunities and a plan.

## What do we know about you?
- **Who you are:** your name or nickname, birth date, WhatsApp number, your governorate and school.
- **Your answers and results:** what you answer in the quizzes, your scores and your career matches. We treat these as **sensitive**, so we are extra careful.
- **How you use the app:** which pages and buttons you use. We use this only to fix problems and make the app better. We don't follow you around the internet.
- **Technical errors:** if the app crashes, we get a report.

## Why do we need your parent or guardian?
You are under 18, and the law says a parent or guardian must say "yes" before we collect and use data like yours. Until they do, you can only try the short teaser and set up your account. We only keep the basics for this step: [nickname, birth date, your WhatsApp number and your parent's number].

## Who sees your data?
| Who | What they see |
|---|---|
| **You** | Everything about you |
| **Your parent or guardian** | A **summary** of your results: top matches, strengths, roadmap progress. **Never your raw answers.** |
| **Your school** (only if your school signed you up) | [Only what the school form says, e.g., whether you finished and a summary] |
| **Our service providers** | Only what they need to run the app (hosting, WhatsApp messages, error tracking). They can't use it for their own purposes. |
| **Anyone else** | No one. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads. |

## The AI part
An AI (Claude, from Anthropic) writes the explanations in your results. It gets your **scores**, not your name, phone number, school or birth date. AI can make mistakes. Your results are **ideas to explore, not a diagnosis and not a rule for your life.**

## Some of your data travels
Our servers are in Europe, and the AI service is in the USA. Your parent is asked to agree to this when they give consent.

## How long do we keep it?
While your account is active. If you or your parent stop it, we delete your data within 30 days [see Privacy Policy for the exceptions]. A small record that your parent said "yes" or "no" is kept [5] years as proof.

## Your rights — you can ask us to
- show you your data;
- fix it;
- delete it;
- stop using it;
- stop the AI texts, or object to how your data is used.

We answer within **6 working days**, and it's free. Write to [privacy@anabokra.example] or go to *Settings → My data*. Your parent can also withdraw consent at any time, and then we stop and delete.

## If something feels wrong
Talk to a parent, teacher or another adult you trust. If you feel very down or unsafe, please don't wait for the app, call a trusted adult or the child helpline [16000 — verify]. If you think we handled your data wrongly, contact us or the Personal Data Protection Center.
