## 1. What anaBokra is
anaBokra is a career guidance app operated by **[anaBokra legal entity name]** ("anaBokra", "we"). You answer questions about your interests, personality, work values and abilities. The app then suggests career groups and careers, and lets you explore study routes in Egypt on a map of islands. Your answers and results are kept **on your device**, not on our servers (see the Privacy Policy).

## 2. Guidance, not diagnosis — please read this
- anaBokra gives **educational and career guidance only**. It is **not** a medical, psychological, psychiatric, clinical or therapeutic service. Nothing on anaBokra is a **diagnosis**, a treatment, or a professional psychological evaluation. **[LAWYER: confirm that Egyptian rules on psychological practice and testing do not apply to our service, and the wording.]**
- The Arabic version of the questions is **being validated** with Egyptian students. Results should be read as indicative.
- Results show **possibilities** based on your answers on a particular day. They can be incomplete or wrong, and they change as you grow. They do not predict your success, and no career is "closed" to you because of a result.
- Information about careers, salaries, universities and admission is for orientation. Check important facts with the official source.
- Do not make big decisions (choosing a track, leaving school, spending money) based only on anaBokra. Talk to your parents, teachers, a school counselor or a qualified professional.
- If you feel anxious, hopeless or unsafe, anaBokra cannot help with that. Please talk to a trusted adult or a qualified professional straight away, or call [the child helpline — number to be verified]. In an emergency contact the emergency services.
