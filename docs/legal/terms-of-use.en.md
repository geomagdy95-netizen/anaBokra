## 1. What anaBokra is
anaBokra is a career guidance app operated by **[anaBokra legal entity name]** ("anaBokra", "we"). You answer questions about your interests, personality, work values and abilities. The app then suggests career groups and careers, and lets you explore the routes into them in Egypt on a map of islands. Your answers and results are kept **on your device**, not on our servers (see the Privacy Policy).

## 2. Guidance, not diagnosis — please read this
- anaBokra gives **educational and career guidance only**. It is **not** a medical, psychological, psychiatric, clinical or therapeutic service. Nothing on anaBokra is a **diagnosis**, a treatment, or a professional psychological evaluation. **[LAWYER: confirm that Egyptian rules on psychological practice and testing do not apply to our service, and the wording.]**

- Information about careers, salaries, study programmes and hiring is for orientation. Check important facts with the official source.
- Do not make big decisions (changing your specialisation, leaving your studies or a job, spending money) based only on anaBokra. Talk to people you trust, a career counsellor or a qualified professional.
