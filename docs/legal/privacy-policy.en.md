## 1. Who we are

anaBokra is operated by **[anaBokra legal entity name]**, [Commercial Register No.], [registered address], Egypt ("anaBokra", "we", "us").

**Data protection contact:** [name] · [privacy@anabokra.example] · [address]. **[LAWYER: whether a registered Data Protection Officer is required for phase 1.]**

## 2. The most important points

1. **Your answers and results stay on your device, encrypted.** We do not receive them and cannot see them.
2. **No account, no email, no AI.** You do not sign up or log in.
3. **Who can use anaBokra:** people aged **15 and over**, on their own. We ask only for an age band, never a name, phone number or ID number.
4. **Anonymous statistics only if you agree.** You can choose to send an anonymous summary that can't be traced to you.
5. We **do not sell** data, show advertising or send marketing.
6. anaBokra gives **guidance, not a diagnosis** (see the Terms of Use).

## 3. Who this policy is about

- People aged 18 and over who use anaBokra, including participants in our university pilot. anaBokra is designed for students and recent graduates aged 18–25. **[LAWYER: ages 18–20 and full civil capacity at 21.]**
- Young people aged 15–17.
- Visitors to our website.

Under-15s may not use anaBokra.

## 4. What the app keeps on your device (encrypted)

| Data | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Profile | Age band (15–17, 18–20, 21–25, 26+), stage (school, university or graduate), language; if you answer them: field of study and year of study or graduation, school system, track and expected score (school only), study budget and governorate; gender only if given (used only for Arabic grammar); a pilot event code if entered | Show your profile and relevant routes |
| Answers | Your answers to the interests, personality, work values and abilities questions | Calculate your scores and career matches |
| Results | Scores, career matches, the islands you explore, progress and points | Show your results and your map |
| Readiness check | Your answers to the short "how ready do you feel" questions | Show how your readiness changes |
| Acceptance | What you accepted, when, which versions, and your anonymous-statistics choice | Remember your choices |
| Nickname and password | The nickname you choose; if you set a password, salted hashes of it and of its recovery code, and a count of wrong tries | Greet you, and lock the app if you want it locked. The password itself is never stored or sent |

All of this is **encrypted on the device** and stays there. Scoring and matching run **inside the app**. We do not receive this data, so we cannot see, correct, recover or delete it for you. You control it (section 8).

## 5. What reaches our servers

### 5.1 Content
The app downloads its content (questions, careers, islands, routes) from our server [hosted at: OVH, country — to be confirmed]. The server receives technical information needed to send content, such as your device's internet (IP) address and browser type. We use it only to deliver content and keep the service secure, do not link it to your answers, and [do not store IP addresses / keep server logs for no more than [7] days — Engineering to confirm].

We do **not** use analytics, advertising tools or crash-reporting tools that send data about you [Engineering to confirm].

### 5.2 Anonymous statistics (only if you agree)
If you agree, the app sends **anonymous records**: **one summary** when an assessment is finished (age band, stage, language, top interest areas and career groups, score bands, and how many parts were completed); **one record for each career path you check** (the path and its band: fits well, partly, or worth rethinking); and, if you answer it, **your 1–5 rating** of whether your results were clear and useful. Each record carries only your age band, stage, language and, if you entered one, the pilot event code. None includes a name, phone number, ID number, device identifier or exact time; the records cannot be linked to each other, and cannot be traced back to you. You can turn it off at any time in Settings. **[LAWYER: confirm that these summaries are anonymous and outside the PDPL, including for 15–17.]**

### 5.3 Research data at pilot sessions (only if you agree)
At a university pilot session, adults can also choose to send their **individual answers without any name or ID** (tagged with the event code, age band, stage and language), including the answers to each path they check with that path's score parts and band, and their readiness answers before and after linked by a **random code made on the phone**, so the change can be measured. **[LAWYER: the linked readiness data is pseudonymous.]** We keep this data until the research analysis is finished, and **no later than 17 June 2027**. Group statistics may be kept. You can stop at any time in the app (*University session*): nothing more is sent, and the readiness answers sent from your phone are deleted using its random code. The individual answers carry no code, so they cannot be picked out; they are deleted with the rest of the research data.

### 5.4 Moving to a new phone
Your data is locked with a passphrase you choose and saved as a **backup file on your device**. You carry it to the new phone yourself; it never passes through us.

## 6. Who receives data

Nobody receives your answers or results from us, because we never have them. Anonymous statistics and research data are used by anaBokra and [researchers working with us] to improve anaBokra and study the Arabic assessment, and are published only as group statistics. A pilot partner (your university or institute) receives only an anonymized group report. Our hosting provider [OVH] processes the data in section 5 on our behalf. We do not sell data or share it for marketing.

## 7. Legal basis

anaBokra does not collect or process your answers, results or profile, so it does not rely on a legal basis for them. For anonymous statistics and research data you choose to send, we rely on **explicit consent** (PDPL Art. 2, 6(1) and 12). **[LAWYER: confirm, including for 15–17 and ages 18–20.]**

## 8. Your choices and rights

- **See** your data: it is all in the app.
- **Change** it: edit your profile or retake a part of the assessment.
- **Delete** it: *Delete all my data* removes everything from the device straight away. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data also removes it.
- **Move** it to a new phone with a backup file (5.4).
- **Turn off** anonymous statistics in Settings at any time.
- **Data already sent:** anonymous summaries and anonymous research answers cannot be traced back to you, so they cannot be removed. Linked readiness answers can be found only with the random code on your phone; [use *Settings → Research data* to ask us to delete them — Product to confirm].
- You can complain to the Personal Data Protection Center (PDPL Art. 33) or go to court.

## 9. Wellbeing

anaBokra's results are suggestions to explore, not a judgement on anyone. If you feel anxious, hopeless or unsafe, please talk to someone you trust or call [a verified helpline — number to be confirmed]. At pilot sessions, your university or institute names a contact you can talk to.

## 10. Local storage and cookies

The app uses your browser's or device's local storage to keep your encrypted data on your device, and essential storage for your language choice. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If we change how data is handled, for example to offer accounts or to store data on our servers, we will update this policy, show the change in the app before it takes effect, and ask for consent where needed. Your on-device data will not be uploaded without your agreement.
