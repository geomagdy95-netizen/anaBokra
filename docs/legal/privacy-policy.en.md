## 2. The most important points

1. **Your answers and results stay on your device, encrypted.** We do not receive them and cannot see them.
2. **No account, no email, no AI.** You do not sign up or log in.
3. **Who can use anaBokra:** people aged 18 and over on their own, and **15–17-year-olds with a parent or guardian** who verifies their phone number and gives their national ID number. Those details are also kept **on the device only, encrypted**.
4. **Anonymous statistics only if you agree.** You (or, for 15–17, your parent) can choose to send an anonymous summary that can't be traced to you.
5. We **do not sell** data, show advertising or send marketing.
6. anaBokra gives **guidance, not a diagnosis** (see the Terms of Use).

## 3. Who this policy is about

- People aged 18 and over who use anaBokra, including participants in our university pilot. anaBokra is designed for students and recent graduates aged 18–25. **[LAWYER: ages 18–20 and full civil capacity at 21.]**
- Young people aged 15–17, and their **parents or legal guardians**.
- Visitors to our website.

Under-15s may not use anaBokra.

## 4. What the app keeps on your device (encrypted)

| Data | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Profile | Age band (15–17, 18–20, 21–25, 26+), stage (school, university or graduate), language; if you answer them: field of study and year of study or graduation, school system, track and expected score (school only), study budget and governorate; gender only if given (used only for Arabic grammar); a pilot event code if entered | Show your profile and relevant routes |
| Answers | Your answers to the interests, personality, work values and abilities questions | Calculate your scores and career matches |
| Results | Scores, career matches, the islands you explore, progress and points | Show your results and your map |
| Readiness check | Your answers to the short "how ready do you feel" questions | Show how your readiness changes |
| Acceptance | What you accepted, when, which versions, and your anonymous-statistics choice | Remember your choices |
| **Parent or guardian (15–17 only)** | Name, relationship, **national ID number**, the last 3 digits of the verified phone number, verification time, a hash of the parent PIN | Record that a verified parent or guardian agreed, and protect deletion and settings with the parent PIN |

All of this is **encrypted on the device** and stays there. Scoring and matching run **inside the app**. We do not receive this data, so we cannot see, correct, recover or delete it for you. You control it (section 8).

**National ID number.** The app checks the number on the device (length, date of birth, governorate code, check digit) and uses the date of birth to confirm the parent or guardian is an adult. The number is **never sent to anaBokra**. This check cannot confirm that the person is the young person's parent; it records the parent's declaration. **[LAWYER: whether collecting the national ID, even on the device only, is allowed and proportionate.]**

## 5. What reaches our servers

### 5.1 Content
The app downloads its content (questions, careers, islands, routes) from our server [hosted at: OVH, country — to be confirmed]. The server receives technical information needed to send content, such as your device's internet (IP) address and browser type. We use it only to deliver content and keep the service secure, do not link it to your answers, and [do not store IP addresses / keep server logs for no more than [7] days — Engineering to confirm].

We do **not** use analytics, advertising tools or crash-reporting tools that send data about you [Engineering to confirm].

### 5.2 Parent phone verification (15–17 only)
To verify the parent's or guardian's phone number, the number is sent to our verification service and an SMS provider [name, Egypt — to be confirmed] **only to send the code**. We keep only what is needed to check the code for 10 minutes and to prevent abuse ([a one-way hash of the number for rate limiting, deleted within [24] hours — Engineering to confirm]); we do not keep the number. **[LAWYER: is this processing, and does it need anything further?]**

### 5.3 Anonymous statistics (only if you agree)
If you agree (for 15–17, if the parent agrees), the app sends **one anonymous summary** when an assessment is finished: age band, stage, language, top interest areas and career groups, score bands, and how many parts were completed. If you entered a pilot event code, the summary carries it. It never includes a name, phone number, ID number, device identifier or exact time, and it cannot be traced back to you. You can turn it off at any time in Settings. **[LAWYER: confirm that these summaries are anonymous and outside the PDPL, including for 15–17.]**

### 5.4 Research data at pilot sessions (only if you agree)
At a university pilot session, adults can also choose to send their **individual answers without any name or ID** (tagged with the event code, age band, stage and language), and their readiness answers before and after linked by a **random code made on the phone**, so the change can be measured. **[LAWYER: the linked readiness data is pseudonymous.]** We keep this data until the research analysis is finished, and **no later than 17 June 2027**. Group statistics may be kept.

### 5.5 Moving to a new phone
- **Backup file:** your data is locked with a passphrase you choose and saved as a file **on your device**. It does not pass through us.
- **Transfer code:** your data is locked on the old phone with a key that never leaves your phones, passes through our server **in a form we cannot read**, and is deleted from the server once received or after 10 minutes. **[LAWYER: is relaying encrypted data we cannot read "processing"?]**

## 6. Who receives data

Nobody receives your answers or results from us, because we never have them. Anonymous statistics and research data are used by anaBokra and [researchers working with us] to improve anaBokra and study the Arabic assessment, and are published only as group statistics. A pilot partner (your university or institute) receives only an anonymized group report. Our hosting provider [OVH] and SMS provider [name] process the data in section 5 on our behalf. We do not sell data or share it for marketing.

## 7. Legal basis

anaBokra does not collect or process your answers, results, profile or the parent's details, so it does not rely on a legal basis for them; the parent's verification and consent for 15–17 is a safeguard and a record of permission. For the parent's phone number during verification (5.2), and for anonymous statistics and research data you choose to send, we rely on **explicit consent** (PDPL Art. 2, 6(1) and 12). **[LAWYER: confirm, including for 15–17 and ages 18–20.]**

## 8. Your choices and rights

- **See** your data: it is all in the app.
- **Change** it: edit your profile or retake a part of the assessment.
- **Delete** it: *Delete all my data* removes everything from the device straight away. For 15–17, the parent PIN is required. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data also removes it.
- **Move** it to a new phone with a backup file or a transfer code (5.5).
- **Turn off** anonymous statistics in Settings at any time.
- **Data already sent:** anonymous summaries and anonymous research answers cannot be traced back to you, so they cannot be removed. Linked readiness answers can be found only with the random code on your phone; [use *Settings → Research data* to ask us to delete them — Product to confirm].
- You can complain to the Personal Data Protection Center (PDPL Art. 33) or go to court.

## 9. Wellbeing

anaBokra's results are suggestions to explore, not a judgement on anyone. If you feel anxious, hopeless or unsafe, please talk to someone you trust or call [a verified helpline — number to be confirmed]. At pilot sessions, your university or institute names a contact you can talk to.

## 10. Local storage and cookies

The app uses your browser's or device's local storage to keep your encrypted data on your device, and essential storage for your language choice. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If we change how data is handled, for example to offer accounts or to store data on our servers, we will update this policy, show the change in the app before it takes effect, and ask for consent where needed. Your on-device data will not be uploaded without your (or your parent's) agreement.
