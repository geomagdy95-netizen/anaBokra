anaBokra is operated by **[anaBokra legal entity name]**, [Commercial Register No.], [registered address], Egypt ("anaBokra", "we", "us").

**Data protection contact:** [name] · [privacy@anabokra.example] · [address]. **[LAWYER: whether a registered Data Protection Officer is required for phase 1.]**

## 2. The most important points

1. **Your answers and results stay on your device.** anaBokra does not send your answers, results or profile to us. We cannot see them.
2. **No account, no phone number, no email, no name.** You do not sign up or log in.
3. **No artificial intelligence.** Result explanations were written by our team in advance and are chosen by the app on your device.
4. **anaBokra is for people aged 18 and over** in this phase.
5. **Research data only if you choose.** At a pilot session you can choose to send your answers **anonymously** to help us check that the Arabic questions work well. Otherwise nothing about you is sent.
6. We **do not sell** data, show advertising or send marketing.
7. anaBokra gives **guidance, not a diagnosis** (see the Terms of Use).

## 3. Who this policy is about

People aged 18 and over who use the anaBokra app, including participants in our university pilot, and visitors to our website. anaBokra is designed for students and recent graduates aged 18–25. **[LAWYER: ages 18–20 and full civil capacity at 21.]**

## 4. What the app keeps on your device

| Data | Examples | Why | Where it is kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| Profile | Age band (18–20, 21–25, 26+), stage (student or graduate), language; gender only if you choose to give it (used only for Arabic grammar); a pilot event code if you entered one | Show your profile and relevant routes | On your device only |
| Answers | Your answers to the interests, personality, work values and abilities questions | Calculate your scores and career matches | On your device only |
| Results | Scores, career matches, the islands you explore, your progress and points | Show your results and your map | On your device only |
| Readiness check | Your answers to the short "how ready do you feel" questions | Show how your readiness changes | On your device only |
| Acceptance | That you accepted the terms, when, which text version, and your research choice | Remember your choices | On your device only |

The scoring and matching run **inside the app on your device**. We do not receive this data, so we cannot see it, correct it, recover it or delete it for you. You control it (section 8).

## 5. What reaches our servers

### 5.1 Content
The app downloads its content (questions, careers, islands, routes) from our server [hosted at: provider, country — to be confirmed]. Like any website, the server receives technical information needed to send that content, such as your device's internet (IP) address and browser type. We use it only to deliver content and keep the service secure, we do not link it to your answers, and we [do not store IP addresses / keep server logs for no more than [7] days — Engineering to confirm]. **[LAWYER: whether this technical data, and the server's location, needs any further step.]**

We do **not** use analytics, advertising tools or crash-reporting tools that send data about you [Engineering to confirm].

### 5.2 Pilot sessions: totals
If you enter a pilot event code, the app sends **totals** for that event (for example how many people started and finished, average ratings, most common career matches). These describe no individual.

### 5.3 Research data, only if you opt in
At a pilot session the app asks whether you want to help validate the Arabic assessment. **Only if you say yes**, it sends:
- your answers **with no name, ID or code**, tagged only with the event code, your age band, your stage and your language; and
- your readiness answers before and after, linked to each other by a **random code made on your phone**, so we can measure the change. **[LAWYER: this linked readiness data is pseudonymous, not anonymous.]**

These go to [server provider, country — to be confirmed]. We keep them until the research analysis is finished, and **no later than 17 June 2027**. Group statistics from the analysis may be kept, since they describe no one. **[LAWYER: confirm that the anonymous answer sets fall outside the PDPL, and what applies to the linked readiness data and to hosting location.]**

Saying no changes nothing about how the app works for you.

## 6. Who receives data

Nobody receives your answers or results from us, because we never have them. Research data you chose to share is used by anaBokra and [researchers working with us] only to study the Arabic assessment, and is published only as group statistics. A pilot partner (your university or institute) receives only an anonymized group report and never anyone's individual results. Our hosting provider [name] stores content and processes the data in section 5 on our behalf. We do not sell data or share it for marketing.

## 7. Legal basis

anaBokra does not collect or process your answers, results or profile, so it does not rely on a legal basis for them. For research data you choose to send, we rely on your **explicit consent** (PDPL Art. 2 and 6(1)), which you can refuse without any effect on the app. **[LAWYER: confirm, including for ages 18–20.]**

## 8. Your choices and rights

- **See** your data: it is all in the app.
- **Change** it: edit your profile or retake a part of the assessment.
- **Delete** it: *Delete all my data* removes everything from your device straight away. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data also removes it.
- **Research data:** if you opted in, anonymous answers already sent cannot be traced back to you, so they cannot be removed. Linked readiness answers can be found only with the random code on your phone; [to ask us to delete them, use *Settings → Research data* before deleting the app — Product to confirm].
- You can complain to the Personal Data Protection Center (PDPL Art. 33) or go to court.

## 9. Wellbeing

anaBokra's results are suggestions to explore, not a judgement on anyone. If you feel anxious, hopeless or unsafe, please talk to someone you trust or call [a verified helpline — number to be confirmed]. At pilot sessions, your university or institute names a contact you can talk to.

## 10. Local storage and cookies

The app uses your browser's or device's local storage to keep your data on your device, and essential storage for your language choice. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If we change how data is handled, for example to offer accounts or to store data on our servers (phase 2), we will update this policy, show the change in the app before it takes effect, and ask for your consent where needed. Your on-device data will not be uploaded without your agreement.
